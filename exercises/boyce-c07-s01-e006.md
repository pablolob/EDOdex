## Enunciado

Si $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 & 0 \\ 3 & 2 & -1 \\ -2 & 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$, $B = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & -1 \\ -2 & 3 & 3 \\ 1 & 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$ y $C = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$, verifique que
(a) $(AB)C = A(BC)$
(b) $(A + B) + C = A + (B + C)$
(c) $A(B + C) = AB + AC$
